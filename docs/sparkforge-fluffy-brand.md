# SparkForge Fluffy Character System\n\nSparkForge uses **Fluffies** as its native character language.\n\nThe existing generic Dot/puffball runtime is the technical rendering foundation. The product-facing identity is Fluffy.\n\n## Ownership and source direction\n\nThe Fluffy character family is an original user-created creative property. SparkForge must treat supplied Fluffy artwork, character sheets, poses, names, expressions, accessories and style references as user-owned source material.\n\nDo not replace the Fluffy identity with generic stock mascots or third-party character assets.\n\n## Character rules\n- Soft, friendly, collectible silhouette.\n- Expressive eyes and readable emotions.\n- Distinctive role-specific accessories.\n- Consistent proportions across the workforce.\n- Brand palette should complement, not overpower, the characters.\n- Characters can evolve by role, pose and accessory while remaining recognizably part of one family.\n- New generated Fluffies should be original variations, not copies of unrelated commercial mascots.\n\n## Workforce\n\n| Agent | Fluffy role | Direction |\n|---|---|---|\n| Scout | Explorer | Research/antenna cues |\n| Forge | Builder | Maker/cap cues |\n| Canvas | Creative | Art/bow cues |\n| Listing | Seller | Communication/headphone cues |\n| Pulse | Growth | Fresh/sprout cues |\n| Audience | Community | Warm/halo cues |\n| Operator | Lead | Confident/chubby silhouette |\n| Browser | Executor | Tech/headphone cues |\n\n## Asset architecture\n\nThe runtime should support three layers:\n\n1. **Native 3D Fluffy** — current procedural renderer for speed and animation.\n2. **Owned 2D Fluffy assets** — supplied/generated character art for hero scenes, cards, onboarding and marketing.\n3. **Generated product imagery** — Canvas uses the canonical Fluffy/brand system when creating mockups and promotional assets.\n\nWhen owned 2D assets are supplied, they should become the visual source of truth for the corresponding character rather than being silently redrawn into an unrelated style.\n\n## Product rule\n\nEvery major SparkForge surface should feel like SparkForge, not a lightly re-skinned generic agent app.\n\nThe Fluffies, SparkForge brand system, creator-business workflow and evidence-first agent behavior are core product identity.
